## Panel-specific overlay

The transformed design is one medium-sized cluster of outline-free flat silhouettes — the subject plus only the few things right beside it — moved close together so they overlap front to back, standing along one invisible horizontal baseline, set slightly to one side of a single deep, grainy, gradient-free dark field with generous empty space all round and more headroom above than below. Every shape is one flat colour seen front-on or in side profile, with soft, slightly hand-cut edges, no internal detail beyond a few same-hue darker cut-ins for openings or gaps, no perspective and no cast shadow; depth reads only from overlap and value. Exactly one shape is lifted to a near-warm-white and reads as lit; the rest are low-saturation pastel greys mixed from the photograph. When the photograph is a scene, keep only its most representative single object or fragment. The one short word is a tiny thin serif uppercase word set in the dark space on the open side, coloured a step softer than the brightest shape and clearly apart from the cluster. Reject a full-scene copy, a filled background, scattered isolated silhouettes, any outline, several equally bright shapes with no focus, three-dimensional lighting, pure black, candy colour, gradients, distressed texture, and over-clean vector edges.

These overlay rules add to `references/soldier-runtime.md`. They never replace the source brief or the family runtime contract.

Write every selected final PNG directly inside one fresh task directory under `~/Desktop/xxd/xxd-panel-345/` (or the explicit `--out` root). Use collision-safe filenames; do not create source, mode, or size subdirectories and do not generate an automatic contact sheet.
